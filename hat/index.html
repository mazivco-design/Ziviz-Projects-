Neighbors
Neighbor counts when turned by

Every hat is a rotated (sometimes mirrored) copy of the same shape. A neighbor only counts if it is turned, relative to this tile, by one of the pressed angles. Turn off some angles and the rules become one-handed.

Start with

Life-style patterns: each tile counts its live neighbors. Numbers after B bring a dead tile to life; numbers after S keep a live tile alive. Sliders and the start choice apply on Restart.

Patch size
5

Each hat is built from 8 kites. Colors follow the metatile each hat belongs to, as in Smith, Myers, Kaplan and Goodman-Strauss (2023). Resizing the patch clears your painting and cells. Patch size 6 has about 54,000 tiles and may be slow on older devices.
